Revenue reconciliation for a multi-brand clinic group

A PE-backed medspa and aesthetic medicine group ran 14 locations under 7 brands across 6 US states. Revenue flowed from the point-of-sale system through Redshift and Snowflake into Power BI, and the reports did not add up.

Type
Client work, contract
When
Apr to Oct 2026
Role
Data Quality and Governance Lead
Focus
Reconciliation, metrics, monitoring

01The problem

Nearly a quarter of revenue, 24%, could not be attributed to the right location or provider, so reports by location and provider could not be reconciled back to the point-of-sale data.

The cause was a join fallback that failed silently, so nothing alerted. The client name is withheld, as the work is confidential.

02What I built

  • Traced $2.97M of misattributed revenue through four pipeline layers to one silent join fallback. Replaced a 19,000-row lookup table with a 233-entry mapping that can actually be reviewed.
  • Found $2.17M of discounts zeroed across 766,000 rows since 2021, followed through three layers, with four alternative source tables ruled out before calling it.
  • Validated 19 metrics against source before release. Three of four builds were rejected in UAT: a 270-day lookback where 90 days was specified, a 300% error from the wrong fact table, and rows counted instead of distinct invoices. A 13% understatement was caught before anyone saw it.
  • Mapped 341 product names into 24 SKU groups across 9 categories, approved by the VP of Strategic Growth.
  • A naming audit found 99 defects, with more than $800K of revenue split across names and $336K still flowing through deprecated services.
  • A 12-check monitoring suite, and a Power BI model on Snowflake behind a weekly report for the VP of Operations.
