Approach

How I work.

01

Every check has an owner

A control without an owner, a severity and a runbook is a warning people learn to ignore.

02

Reconcile to the source

Totals that agree with themselves can still be wrong. I trace numbers back to where they came from until they close.

03

Rules as code

Privacy, access and quality rules belong in the pipeline, tested on every build, not in a document nobody reads.
